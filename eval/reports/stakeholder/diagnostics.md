BGE cosine scores are compressed into a high band (BAAI's model card says so), and mean query-passage similarity in this run is 0.75, so `SUPPORT_THRESHOLD = 0.50` sits below almost any in-domain pair and passes topical sentences that assert things the passage does not say.
