What this evaluation cannot tell you

  • There are no gold reference answers. Faithfulness here means supported by what was retrieved, not true; correctness of local claims needs a domain reviewer.
  • Expected sources and probes are provisional labels written from catalogue titles. Low recall may mean the label is wrong, not the retrieval.
  • Groundedness is an embedding-similarity check. It catches topical invention but can pass a sentence that reverses a document's meaning.
  • Generation is sampled at temperature 0.2, so this is one draw. Re-run before treating small differences as real.
  • EMS response, freight and transit first/last-mile have thin corpus coverage. A good answer there is an honest gap statement, which relevancy and probe metrics under-credit.
