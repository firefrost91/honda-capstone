How each group's questions fared

Each group asked three questions of its own. A question "passes" when it clears every check: the right documents were found, no made-up figures, the answer fits the question, and it states what the evidence does not cover.

Read with care

Limits of this test

  • There are no "correct" model answers to compare against. "Grounded" means the answer matches the documents it found, not that it is true. A domain expert still needs to review the local claims.
  • The list of documents each question should find was drafted from document titles and has not been reviewed. Some "missed" documents may be wrong expectations rather than retrieval failures.
  • With 34 questions, differences of less than about 10 points are not meaningful. The model also varies a little from run to run.
